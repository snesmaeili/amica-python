Removed redundant transcendental evaluations from the density and accumulator hot loops: `logsumexp` already forms an exponential that was then recomputed, and the per-mixture loop took three logarithms and three exponentials for two distinct values. Added profiling tools for where a fit's time (`amica.benchmark.profile_cpu`), memory (`profile_memory`) and scaling (`profile_scaling`) go, and `scripts/regression_vs_ref.py`, which fits identical data under two checkouts and reports the matched unmixing correlation and log-likelihood against a chosen baseline commit.
